## Limits

- The organiser gateway has produced malformed content in some earlier runs. Strict validation and bounded repair reject it; the clean normal and sales runs above prove successful paths, not a measured availability service-level objective. No token, live cost, or latency distribution has been measured for submission.
- Post-purchase contingency is a bounded approved synthetic case, not an open-ended purchasing optimiser. Its result accounts only for new purchase cash and preserves existing commitments.
- Late `SALES_UPDATED` materiality cannot reopen the approved `CASH_SLICE_V1` issue window. The safe result is `CALCULATION_INCOMPLETE`; post-purchase contingency is the supported additional-purchase path in its declared domain.
- No hosted URL, second-machine connection, AWS/Bedrock verification, video capture, external submission, or real business outcome is claimed. The organiser gateway is the worker's live model path; OpenClaw is an isolated smoke-test path and is not the application worker.
